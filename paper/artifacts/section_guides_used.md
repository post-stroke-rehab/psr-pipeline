## Workflow Applied

1. **Master-cai workflow:** start with a paper story, draft section by section, maintain a claim-evidence map, then reverse-outline and self-review.
2. **K-Dense literature-review guidance:** prioritize primary sources, search broadly enough to cover reviews, datasets, and recent stroke-specific decoding work, and synthesize thematically rather than listing papers one by one.
3. **K-Dense scientific-writing guidance:** keep full prose paragraphs, use IMRAD structure, and rewrite the technical material as a manuscript rather than as notes.
4. **Reviewer-style pass:** actively look for overclaiming, unsupported deployment statements, and misleading cross-paper comparisons.
