## Synthesis decisions

- Use literature thematically rather than as a study-by-study catalog.
- Keep Ninapro in the paper because it is the dominant healthy-subject benchmark family and appears directly in recent stroke-transfer work.
- Keep PhysioMio central because it is the dataset actually used by the project and is stroke-specific, bilateral, and longitudinal.
- Avoid direct superiority claims across papers because gesture vocabularies, sensor setups, populations, and evaluation metrics differ substantially.
